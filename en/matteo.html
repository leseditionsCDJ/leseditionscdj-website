Gisele the Stargazing Giraffe

Matteo Collection • Ages 4–7

Gisele loves watching the stars and spending time looking up at the sky.

But one day, she arrives at Matteo's veterinary clinic with a sore neck. Looking up at the sky hurts.

Matteo helps her understand what is happening while they discover fascinating facts about giraffes and their amazing long necks.

A gentle and educational adventure about giraffes, stargazing and the importance of taking care of yourself.
