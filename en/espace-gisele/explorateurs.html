🦒 The tallest animal on land!

The giraffe is the tallest living land animal in the world. An adult male is often more than 5 metres tall.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

👅 An amazingly long tongue!

A giraffe’s tongue is about 45 to 50 cm long — almost as long as a young child’s arm!

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

🌿 A tongue that grabs leaves!

Its very flexible tongue and lips help it grab leaves from trees, even between thorns.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

🍃 It chews its meal again!

A giraffe is a ruminant. After swallowing food, it can bring some of it back up to its mouth and chew it again.

Source: Animal Diversity Web — University of Michigan

👑 Those aren’t really horns!

They are called ossicones. They are bony structures covered with skin and hair. Adult males can use them during necking fights.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

🏃 A very fast giant!

Even though it is very tall, a giraffe can run at around 50 km/h.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

😴 Lots of little naps!

In a zoo study, adult giraffes slept about 4½ hours in 24 hours, often in many short naps.

Source: Tobler & Schwierin — scientific giraffe sleep study

👶 A long wait for a calf!

A mother giraffe is pregnant for about 15 months. She usually gives birth to one calf.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

🐾 Up on its feet quickly!

A giraffe calf can stand during its first hour of life and begin moving around very quickly.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation

👥 Groups that keep changing!

Giraffes do not always stay in the same group. Some join, some leave, and new groups form later.

Source: Giraffe Conservation Foundation
