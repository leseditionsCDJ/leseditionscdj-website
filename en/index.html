About Les Éditions Cap Découvertes Jeunesse

Stories that open up the world

Les Éditions Cap Découvertes Jeunesse was born from a simple idea: children should be able to learn while enjoying a great story.

We create children's stories where adventure, humor and discovery come together. Every book is designed to spark children's curiosity, inspire a sense of wonder and help them discover new things about the world around them.

We believe a great story can make children laugh, surprise them and capture their imagination... while also teaching them something real. That's why our books place just as much importance on characters and adventure as they do on the knowledge woven naturally into each story.

Our Mission

To create books that give children the joy of reading and the desire to keep discovering.
